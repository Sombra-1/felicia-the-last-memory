# FELICIA — Phase 7.1 art-direction system

## Product and experience

FELICIA: The Last Memory is a single-session, full-viewport experimental 3D narrative
about choosing the order in which an artificial consciousness recovers Identity,
Fear, and Hope. The approved three-trial mechanics, duration, prose density, state
machine, and all-six-order ending logic must remain unchanged.

The visual pass must make the project feel like a premium interactive artwork, never
a WebGL prototype. Geometry count is not the goal. Each state needs a single hero
subject, a clear interaction target, foreground/middle/background separation,
distinct material language, controlled environmental motion, and a screenshot-worthy
resolved composition.

## Shared material language

- Memory shell: layered transparent surface; separate front/back response; Fresnel
  edge; slow internal gradient; controlled normal distortion; soft depth coloration;
  accent supplied by memory profile. Never indiscriminate expensive transmission.
- Energy filament: tapered luminous ribbon, animated directional flow, fading trail,
  restrained selective bloom, profile-specific rhythm.
- Architecture: blackened metallic or smoked-glass body, subtle roughness variation,
  readable rim/edge illumination, enough local light response to avoid flat silhouette.
- Memory field: temporary procedural transition surface only, with fragment-controlled
  color, flow, and displacement.
- No default-white primitives, permanent wireframes, repeated rectangles, generic
  rings, or featureless glowing spheres.

## Scene composition

- FELICIA and each trial target must read at ordinary laptop brightness.
- Preserve negative space but avoid large empty black regions.
- Use depth attenuation, local rim lights, emissive accents, fake contact-darkening,
  selective foreground silhouettes, and particles only near meaningful light volumes.
- No shadow maps. No broad uniform exposure lift.
- Bloom is selective and controlled. Vignette, grain, haze, and chromatic separation
  are subtle and temporary.

## Identity — mirrored signal corridor

Hero subject: a structured signal nucleus within a black-silver corridor. Mirrored
architecture repeats into depth. Thin bright axes reflect across several surfaces.
Translucent FELICIA echoes converge continuously with player alignment.

Incorrect = fractured reflection offsets. Near = silhouettes converge and planes gain
clarity. Success = one precise symmetry snap and permanent corridor clarification.
Use restrained duplication/refraction illusion and narrow volumetric-style light
slices. Never plain white slabs or a white-ball target.

## Fear — defensive organism

Hero subject: FELICIA’s readable core enclosed by segmented translucent shield petals.
Dark violet body, inner scar coloration, and directional warning light. Incoming wave
fronts must visibly travel toward the core. Correct input rotates/unfolds/thickens the
relevant petal before impact; impact deforms the shield material and produces a
controlled ripple. Late defense leaves a subtle permanent scar. Enclosure tightens
per stage without jump scares, rapid flash, or violent shake.

## Hope — living signal garden

Hero subject: a detailed deforming signal seed with a luminous trail. Branching gold
filaments preserve the player’s path; distant vertical depth and rising particles
lead upward. Organic shell gates anticipate the signal, unfold as it approaches,
emit one harmonic pulse, and remain as growth structures. Previous successful paths
grow the environment. Warmth stays restrained against the dark world.

## Persistent FELICIA anatomy

Consequences are anatomical transformations, never overlay symbols.

- First memory is the largest structural change.
- Identity: shell symmetry, mirrored ribs, stable internal axis, precise pulse.
- Fear: protective plates around vulnerable regions, embedded violet scars, defensive
  contraction.
- Hope: upward-opening shell layers, internal gold filaments, rising aperture
  particles, wider breathing.
- Second memory modifies the foundation; third completes it.

## Transitions

No scene swap or complete fade. The selected fragment becomes the destination world.
Camera follows one readable path while geometry, light, and materials continuously
reorganize; arrival settles before input unlocks. Returns compress trial material
directly into FELICIA’s anatomy.

- Identity: axes extend, mirror planes unfold, camera crosses a reflective surface,
  FELICIA briefly recedes as reflection.
- Fear: shield petals close around camera, violet aperture becomes chamber, first wave
  is already visible beyond it.
- Hope: filament links fragment/FELICIA/camera, camera follows it upward, chamber trails
  become gates and vertical architecture.

## Signature reconstruction

FELICIA is centered or deliberately offset with a clean silhouette. No foreground
columns block the core. All three memory streams read simultaneously; foundation
stream dominates. Consequences partially detach, liquefy/unfold into streams, pass
through shell layers, ignite internal filaments, reshape the shell, and grow the
environment outward. Camera pulls back only after internal transformation begins.
Architecture frames from behind. Final composition must read at 320px thumbnail size.

## Interface

The WebGL artwork owns the viewport. Keep the existing accessible text, but express
gameplay as compact edge-aligned metadata, short temporary hints, tiny progress marks,
and world-space target feedback. Remove permanent full-width bordered instruction
panels. Technical metadata uses the existing mono stack; emotional statements use the
existing light display stack. Colors remain silver, restrained violet, and tempered
gold. Do not introduce new fonts, colors, rounded-card idioms, or dashboard chrome.

## Responsive and accessibility

Desktop target: 1366×768 and 1440×900. Mobile targets: 360×800, 390×844, 430×932.
Honor safe areas and 44px touch targets. Trial hero and active target stay legible
behind compact controls. Reduced motion preserves every state and interaction through
shorter transforms and opacity/material changes. Low quality preserves silhouette,
target visibility, and material differentiation.
