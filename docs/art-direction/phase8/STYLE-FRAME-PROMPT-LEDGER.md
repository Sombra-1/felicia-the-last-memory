# Phase 8 style-frame prompt ledger

Generation mode: built-in image generation, `stylized-concept`.

All frames were generated as one reference-linked cinematic series. Frame 01
established FELICIA and the cathedral; every subsequent call included the preceding
series images as identity and world references.

## Shared prompt

> State-of-the-art cinematic 3D concept art for a landscape 16:9 experimental
> narrative game. Preserve the same FELICIA: faceted synthetic face, elongated
> suspended torso, narrow sternum, paired thick translucent glass organ-shell lobes,
> ivory heart-organ, articulated black-metal vertebrae, purposeful neural paths, and
> one living asymmetry. Preserve the same monumental black memory cathedral. Use only
> black cathedral stone-metal, translucent memory glass, neural light, and embedded
> scar material. Exactly one hero, one interaction target, and one dominant
> environmental gesture. No text, UI, logos, watermark, orbit rings, circular frames,
> repeated arches, random rods, floating rectangles, line networks, particle clutter,
> or featureless glowing spheres.

## 01 — The Synthetic Saint

> Low distant monumental camera. FELICIA suspended in a near-black cathedral between
> two broad architectural masses. One cold ivory neural pulse inside her sternum and
> one narrow vertical grazing shaft reveal impossible scale. Partial shell visibility,
> thick glass edges, faint violet undertone, strong thumbnail silhouette, controlled
> discovery.

## 02 — Imposed Symmetry

> Exact centered perspective. Two colossal cathedral walls hinge inward and become
> imperfect silver mirror glass. FELICIA’s paired organs are pulled toward unnatural
> symmetry. A broken three-part silver vertebra inside her sternum is the active
> target. Fractured reflections converge toward one cold axial lock.

Output:
[`02-identity-imposed-symmetry-revised.png`](./style-frames/02-identity-imposed-symmetry-revised.png)

Gate revision edit:

> Preserve FELICIA, the cathedral, material language, and exact centered camera.
> Replace the ambiguous luminous bands with exactly three large broken silver
> vertebral blocks: top and bottom centered, middle visibly displaced and rotated,
> separated by two dark fracture gaps with glow confined to the broken faces. Cant
> both mirror-cathedral walls inward until they visibly compress FELICIA's paired
> organ lobes, and use only a few large reflections to reinforce bilateral
> convergence.

> organ physically extends and locks as the 60% foundation. A smaller curved violet

Gate revision edit:

> Preserve the established anatomy, cathedral, materials, and intimate camera.
> Remove competing wire tangles, small branches, and decorative micro-components.
> Organize the exposed body around one large faceted ivory synchronization node with
> three unmistakable docking collars. Make the broad silver vertebral foundation
> dominate roughly 60% of the visual weight, the single curved violet defensive
> organ roughly 25%, and the small gold neural branch roughly 15%, with generous dark
> negative space separating all three insertion paths.
