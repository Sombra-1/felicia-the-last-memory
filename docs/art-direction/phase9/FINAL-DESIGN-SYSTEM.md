# Phase 9 final design system — The Last Fold

## One-sentence visual thesis

FELICIA is the one impossible fold in a continent of dark memory strata: every
choice imposes a physical law on the same continuous matter until consciousness
turns the whole world inside out.

## Central world metaphor

The world is a single suspended field of laminated memory matter extending far
beyond the frame. It behaves like a cross between mineral strata, heavy cloth, and
living tissue, but it is never represented as literal fabric, landscape, anatomy,
or machinery. Its deep folds create foreground occlusion, navigable ravines,
compression chambers, and distant scale.

FELICIA is not an object placed in that field. She is the field's unresolved fold:
one off-axis, load-bearing sweep that refuses to settle flat. Identity pleats the
matter into exact phase. Fear drives a compression fault through it. Hope
delaminates it into an opening. The same surfaces persist, deform, and become the
next state.

This thesis deliberately replaces Phase 8 recovery's optical cathedral, mantle,
memory rail, organ ports, and anatomical extraction.

## FELICIA silhouette

FELICIA begins as a broken calligraphic torsion:

- one thick, asymmetric lower sweep carries visual weight from lower left;
- one thinner counterfold rises behind it toward upper right;
- a deliberate missing span leaves the form structurally unresolved;
- a deep interior cleft exposes articulated tension vanes without forming a torso;
- the outer edge changes thickness continuously and never reads as a primitive;
- the silhouette is cropped by the frame at opening scale, then revealed by camera
  withdrawal;
- no bilateral symmetry, head, face, limb, wing, thorax, body core, or orbit exists.

The initial body is tense, partially occluded, visibly load-bearing, and alive only
through one slow internal propagation. Recovered memories close, protect, or open
the missing span and permanently alter posture, balance, internal rhythm, surface
response, and the surrounding field.

## Form language

- Dominant form: broad, tapered, bevelled lamina with variable edge thickness.
- Secondary form: seated tension vane grown between two existing surfaces.
- Openings: carved clefts, lifted seams, and protected negative-space pockets.
- Joins: compressed saddles and dovetailed seats, never rods or floating joints.
- Repetition: only when Identity imposes it, with controlled variation and clear
  termination.
- Curves: long directional sweeps with one decisive inflection, not decorative
  loops.
- Surface detail: sparse directional scoring that follows stress and propagation.
- Complexity comes from deformation, overlap, and changing thickness rather than
  added props.

Every visible mesh must read as authored after silhouette, taper, bevel, bend,
asymmetry, joining, displacement, and surface treatment. A mesh that remains
recognizable as an untouched sphere, torus, box, tube, plane, or line is removed.

## Spatial language

The composition uses four depth bands:

1. a cropped foreground fold that occludes 8–25% of the frame;
2. FELICIA or the current action target in the midground;
3. one dominant world gesture crossing depth;
4. a quiet, distant stratum that establishes scale.

Shots avoid a centered frontal tableau:

- Awakening: close cropped edge detail; the camera discovers the whole fold.
- Chamber: off-axis three-quarter view with FELICIA crossing the center, not
  sitting on it.
- Identity: compressed oblique view down converging pleats.
- Fear: low, close view from inside the protected negative-space pocket.
- Hope: vertical passage through a seam opening toward newly visible distance.
- Reconstruction: macro detail travels along a transformation front, then an
  extreme pullback reveals the entire field participating.
- Ending: a distant black silhouette against the only full exposure change.

Every shot has one hero, one action target, one environmental gesture, and
restrained atmosphere.

## Material bible

Four primary material families are permitted.

### 1. Charred mineral laminate — structural

- Near-black blue-grey mass with broad roughness variation.
- Layered edge bands expose physical thickness.
- Low-amplitude directional vertex relief breaks broad highlights.
- Grazing light produces narrow travelling highlights while front faces absorb.
- Bevelled profiles, deep overlaps, and self-occlusion prevent black-panel flatness.
- Response is slow and heavy; deformation settles with visible inertia.

### 2. Living opaline membrane — FELICIA

- Smoke-ivory body with restrained blue-violet absorption.
- Separate front, inner, and rear layers provide readable thickness without costly
  physical transmission.
- Edge response uses a clamped view-dependent lift, not a full Fresnel glow.
- Internal gradients travel along the fold direction and pause at stress points.
- Roughness and opacity vary across the surface; no flat purple transparency.
- Response is elastic but never floating.

### 3. Seated nickel cartilage — articulated interior

- Dark nickel with narrow controlled highlights and visible joint seating.
- Tension vanes use custom tapered profiles joined into the cleft.
- Directional light travels from saddle to saddle after successful input.
- Small irregular offsets prevent diagrammatic perfection until Identity acts.
- Response is quick, exact, and mechanically settled.

### 4. Tempered memory strata — persistent consequence

- Not separate decoration: memory changes the actual roughness, relief, thickness,
  and stress direction of structural or living surfaces.
- Identity creates cold lamination seams and controlled highlight travel.
- Fear creates embedded violet-black compression scars and thicker protected edges.
- Hope creates warm internal delamination and exposes new rear surfaces.
- Propagation visibly spreads, stabilizes, and remains for every later state.

Color never carries material identity by itself.

## Color progression

- Awakening: `#030506`, `#090c0f`, smoke ivory `#d8d6cd`, trace blue
  `#39444d`.
- Identity: nickel `#8f9aa1`, cold white `#dfe8e9`, black-blue `#11191d`.
- Fear: bruise violet `#68466f`, deep aubergine `#211823`, protected ivory
  `#cfc8bc`; violet never floods the frame.
- Hope: oxidized amber `#b9823f`, warm ivory `#ead9b9`, distant dawn
  `#786b58`; warmth originates inside the fold.
- Reconstruction: nearly colorless before ordered memory influence arrives at
  60% / 25% / 15%.
- Rebirth: pale mineral daylight `#c9c6b8` and vast blue-black distance; this is
  the only full-scene exposure transformation.

## Lighting progression

### Awakening

1. A narrow internal pulse catches the interior cleft.
2. A rear edge light traces only the unresolved silhouette.
3. A moving graze reveals surface thickness and edge bands.
4. A distant vertical bounce establishes world scale.
5. The three actionable stress sites appear sequentially.

### Identity

One exact lateral source travels down the converging pleats. Successful alignment
produces a sharp, brief nickel lock, then a calm low-saturation reflection. Each
success removes one irregular highlight and seats one vane.

### Fear

A hard threat source advances across the field and is interrupted by existing
folds. The protected cleft retains a narrow ivory highlight. Impact embeds
violet-black damage in the actual surface; surrounding light compresses rather than
turning purple.

### Hope

Warmth begins inside FELICIA's cleft, follows a visible route through overlapping
depth, and exposes progressively farther strata. The background opens only after
the third guidance event.

### Reconstruction and rebirth

Reconstruction begins almost colorless. The foundation owns approximately 60% of
light direction, silhouette, motion, material state, and world response. The second
memory modifies approximately 25%; the third introduces a 15% contradiction. The
rebirth pullback is the sole full-scene exposure lift.

## Camera grammar

- 32–46 mm-equivalent perspective; wide only for the final scale reveal.
- Curved paths with motivated target shifts; no linear dolly or teleport.
- Anticipation begins with a 2–5% counter-move.
- The camera follows the transformation front through existing material.
- Arrival settles in position and target before critical text appears.
- Mobile uses a farther target and stronger depth compression, not a miniature
  desktop crop.
- Controlled pointer parallax is limited to the ending inspection.
- Reduced motion preserves composition using shorter positional changes and light
  propagation instead of cuts.

## Movement grammar

- Base life is one slow internal propagation followed by stillness, not constant
  floating.
- Identity: precise anticipation, synchronized acceleration, decisive lock, zero
  drift at rest.
- Fear: interrupted advance, abrupt compression, elastic protection, heavy
  settlement.
- Hope: continuous lift, progressive unfolding, overshoot into newly created
  space, soft settlement.
- Each input creates immediate local response, then material propagation, world
  acknowledgement, and rest.
- No random rotation, ambient bobbing, unrelated simultaneous motion, or
  never-ending decorative loop.

## Transition grammar

One authoritative `TransitionDirector` owns the sequence:

1. anticipation in camera, sound, and the active surface;
2. visible player-triggered stress;
3. propagation through the existing fold;
4. geometric deformation and material response;
5. camera passage beside or through the transformed matter;
6. destination revealed forming ahead;
7. physical arrival and light settlement;
8. input unlock and text reveal.

Existing world groups remain mounted. Phase is represented by shared deformation
runtime, not visibility swapping, unrelated cross-fades, full-scale spawning, or
bloom concealment. Interrupted timelines settle deterministically through the
existing watchdog and visibility restoration.

## One-world trials

### Identity — imposed pleating

The three alignments rotate and translate actual nested laminae until their scored
stress directions share one vanishing axis. The interaction target is the one
misaligned seam catching exact cold light. Success seats a tension vane, propagates
a silver lock through FELICIA, and leaves the world more constrained.

### Fear — compression fault

A pressure ridge moves through the same strata toward FELICIA's exposed cleft.
The player selects one of three existing overhangs to thicken and intercept it.
Impact deforms that fold, protects internal nickel highlights, and leaves an
embedded bruise-scar plus permanent asymmetrical compression.

### Hope — delamination path

Warm internal propagation must be guided through three lifted seams in the same
field. Each success peels a passage from existing laminate, exposes a farther depth
layer, and lengthens FELICIA's rising counterfold. The final seam reveals distance
that was physically unavailable before.

## UI hierarchy

The WebGL world owns the viewport.

1. One current narrative or action line.
2. One large, immediately operable action target.
3. Three-beat progress.
4. Compact chosen order only when causality matters.
5. FELICIA wordmark, sound, reduced-motion, and quality controls at quiet edges.

No footer telemetry, archive-status decoration, dashboard frame, stacked panel, or
tiny metadata survives. Text appears after scene settlement, stays in negative
space, and never covers FELICIA, the interaction target, a transformation front, or
a critical depth cue.

## Reconstruction metaphor — turning the field inside out

Memory order creates consciousness by deciding how one continuous field can fold.

- The synchronization gesture does not fill a bar. Holding tension slows an
  unstable travelling crease until it seats into the world; releasing lets it
  recoil.
- The first memory establishes the governing folding law across the whole field.
- The second changes friction, timing, and how the fold propagates.
- The third introduces a deliberate break that makes the final consciousness
  individual rather than perfect.
- FELICIA's missing span is not docked or assembled. The field itself passes through
  the cleft, turns inside out, and makes absence load-bearing.
- Light, deformation, internal seating, silhouette, and audio phase-lock all
  respond directly to synchronization.
- Rebirth reveals that the apparent enclosure was the underside of a far larger
  surface; the final profile now shapes the horizon.

No egg, icon, tube, equal stream, particle convergence, docking UI, surrounding
arch, or static central subject is permitted.

## Final-profile rules

All profiles must remain distinguishable as flat black silhouettes.

### Identity-first — the plumb fold

- Tall, narrow, exact, and formally constrained.
- Nested laminae close into one vertical stepped blade.
- The protected cleft becomes a predictable narrow aperture.
- Motion locks on one axis and the distant field obeys one pleating interval.
- Fear adds one compressed shoulder; Hope opens one controlled terminal seam.

### Fear-first — the shelter fold

- Broad, low, wounded, and asymmetrical through survival.
- One heavy overhang curls around a vulnerable negative-space pocket.
- The world compresses toward that pocket and stops.
- Identity seats the shelter with one exact buttress; Hope creates one escape seam.

### Hope-first — the horizon fold

- Tall, expanded, visibly open, and directionally unfinished.
- The counterfold rises beyond the original boundary and creates a huge aperture.
- A new distant layer moves through the opening.
- Identity adds a repeating cadence; Fear thickens and scars one exposed edge.

Second and third memories must change the dominant silhouette, not appear as small
accessories.

## Audio metaphor

- Structural laminate: filtered midrange friction and low wooden-mineral impacts.
- Opaline membrane: narrow harmonic breath with slow internal phase movement.
- Nickel cartilage: short articulated strikes and directional resonant travel.
- Tempered memory: Identity adds measured intervals, Fear interrupts decay, Hope
  opens the upper midrange and stereo depth.
- Every transformation has anticipation, movement, arrival, decay, and audible
  rest.
- Foundation weighting alters later timing and resonance at 60% / 25% / 15%.
- Reconstruction resolves the three systems into one profile-specific cadence.
- The mix prioritizes laptop-speaker midrange, limiter headroom, one ambience
  lifecycle, and no trailer-style music.

## Explicit rejection list

Reject any implementation or review frame containing:

- cathedral, nave, arch, altar, or sacred-machine staging;
- organ anatomy, sternum, rib cage, spinal rail, docking port, or extracted organ;
- humanoid, mannequin, angel, robot woman, face, mask, or torso;
- insect, butterfly, wing pair, thorax, or chrysalis;
- egg, sphere, orb, floating core, or glowing object with surrounding props;
- ring, halo, torus, rod forest, tube bundle, panel stack, technical diagram, or
  particle convergence;
- centered object on black, bilateral symmetry, frontal camera, or equal side props;
- untouched primitives, thin rectangles, transparent flat planes, isolated glowing
  lines, floating icons, repeated frames, or decorative filler;
- cyberpunk neon, uniform purple, uniform gold, global exposure fixes, or
  bloom-dependent readability;
- separate minigame rooms, group swaps, unrelated cross-fades, full-scale spawns,
  or static-subject scene changes;
- constant floating, random rotation, linear camera travel, unmotivated loops, or
  motion without settlement;
- telemetry, dashboard framing, repeated labels, and text covering spatial cues.

If the rendered work resembles any item above at thumbnail scale or in motion, the
change is rejected and revised or reverted.

## Technically achievable implementation plan

- Keep the Phase 8 recovery state machine, three-trial gameplay, active
  synchronization, six-order logic, accessibility, audio lifecycle, diagnostics,
  responsive quality tiers, and authoritative `TransitionDirector`.
- Reuse the existing custom `Shape` / `ExtrudeGeometry`, curve, shader-patching,
  instancing, GSAP, and React Three Fiber patterns already installed.
- Replace the cathedral/organ composition inside the existing recovery scene
  modules with a shared field of a few custom extruded laminae and one persistent
  Phase 9 FELICIA fold.
- Drive chamber, trial, return, reconstruction, and ending from the existing
  mutable runtimes so geometry stays mounted and deforms continuously.
- Limit primary materials to the four families above; use vertex color, procedural
  roughness, clamped view response, layered shells, and travelling shader masks.
- Use a few large forms and instanced secondary vanes; no models, stock textures,
  new runtime dependency, physical transmission, or shadow maps.
- Re-author camera targets and light positions in the current central configuration
  modules.
- Simplify the DOM overlay in place while retaining native buttons, focus order,
  touch target sizes, live regions, reduced motion, low quality, and replay.
- Extend existing capture and Playwright helpers for Phase 9 frame, motion, mobile,
  low-quality, public-production, and three-cycle evidence.
- Keep scene budgets near the recovery baseline: under 70 draw calls, under 45,000
  triangles in steady states, capped DPR, no duplicate loops, and no source maps.

This system is achievable directly in real-time WebGL and does not depend on
photorealistic style-frame fidelity.
